# KAIROS brand brief

**Tagline:** Private signal. Public policy.

## Three key messages

1. Commit your market view without publishing its side or salt.
2. Verify the result and treasury rules through Midnight proofs and public accounting.
3. Explore the Garage on Preprod; trade activity is public and the resolver is trusted with openings.

## Visual identity

Use the existing [KAIROS emblem](../public/kairos-logo.png) and Garage artwork. Keep the industrial workshop, warm brass accents and readable cream text. Do not replace the supplied artwork.

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Coal | `#17110b` | Background |
| Gold | `#e5ad59` | Actions and accents |
| Rust | `#a45b32` | Secondary accents |
| Cream | `#fff1d6` | High-contrast text |

Gold and rust match the app palette; coal and cream are supporting brand choices.
